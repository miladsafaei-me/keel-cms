Learn · Variation 3 Glossary / Key Terms A jargon-buster grid; click a term to expand its plain-language definition.
Bond yield

The return an investor earns on a bond. When bond prices rise, yields fall — and vice versa.

Inference

The stage where a trained AI model produces an answer, as opposed to the training that built it.

Stopgap bill

Short-term legislation that keeps the government funded while a full budget is negotiated.

Deep field

A long-exposure image of a tiny patch of sky that reveals extremely faint, distant galaxies.

Soft landing

When a central bank cools inflation without tipping the economy into a recession.

Ceasefire

An agreed halt to fighting, often temporary, to allow talks or humanitarian access.
